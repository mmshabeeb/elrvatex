This Shipping and Delivery Policy outlines the electronic fulfillment, turnaround schedules, and handover procedures for all services and digital goods purchased through ELEVATEX NOW BUSINESS SERVICE ("ElevateX Now").

📦 Electronic / Digital Fulfillment Notice

ElevateX Now Business Service provides purely digital engineering services, software applications, marketing management, and corporate advisory solutions. We do not sell or ship physical consumer goods. Accordingly, no physical shipping via courier or postal parcel is required or conducted. All deliverables are dispatched electronically via secure digital infrastructure.

1. Delivery Methods & Channels

All digital products, bespoke software code, assets, and service documentation are delivered securely through the following authorized channels:

  • Source Code & Repositories: Private GitHub or GitLab repository invitations, transferring full repository administration upon milestone sign-off.
  • Cloud Deployments: Direct deployment to client-owned cloud environments (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Vercel, Supabase, or Docker containers).
  • Mobile App Builds: Apple TestFlight internal testing tracks for iOS and Google Play Console Internal / Closed Testing tracks for Android.
  • Official Certificates & Legal Documents: Digitally signed PDF documents, MCA incorporation certificates, GST registration certificates, and Trademark Registry filing challans dispatched via encrypted email to the client's registered email address.
  • Design Assets: Collaborative Figma links, vector SVGs, and high-resolution brand asset bundles delivered via secure cloud drives.
  • Invoices & Receipts: Official GST-compliant tax invoices sent automatically via email upon payment confirmation.

3. Shipping / Delivery Charges

Since all deliverables are transmitted digitally via internet protocols, there are no shipping or courier fees applicable to any transaction on elevatexnow.com. All pricing displayed or quoted is inclusive of digital delivery costs.

4. Confirmation & Proof of Delivery

Delivery of a digital service or software milestone is deemed formally complete when any of the following verifiable events occur:

  • The delivery of Git commit pushes or repository ownership transfer to the client's designated GitHub/GitLab account.
  • The transmission of staging server access links or production deployment logs.
  • The successful distribution of an Apple TestFlight build or Google Play Store internal track release.
  • The email delivery of government-issued registration certificates, challans, or analytical marketing performance reports to the client's documented email.

5. Delays & Exceptional Circumstances

While ElevateX Now strives to meet all milestone delivery dates punctually, delivery timelines may occasionally be influenced by:

  • Delays in client approval of wireframes, third-party API credentials, or KYC identity verification documents.
  • Government portal downtimes or unexpected processing backlogs at the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) or Trade Marks Registry.
  • App Store / Google Play Store editorial review review periods (typically 24 to 72 hours, managed by Apple and Google).

In any such event, our project management team will inform the client in writing within 24 hours of identifying the delay and provide a revised estimated completion schedule.
