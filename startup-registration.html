Why Register Now

The Cost of Waiting
is Higher Than You Think

Unregistered founders face personal liability, can't raise funding, can't invoice corporates legally, and can't protect their brand name. Here's what registration unlocks immediately.

01

Limited Liability

Your personal assets — your home, savings, car — are fully shielded from business debts and legal disputes. Without registration, they're not.

02

Investor-Ready

No serious angel investor or VC will wire money to an unregistered entity. Registration is the minimum viable condition for raising capital.

03

Corporate Clients

Large companies require a GSTIN and company registration before they'll sign a vendor agreement with you. Registration opens those doors permanently.

04

Brand Protection

An incorporated entity can file trademarks, copyright its assets, and take legal action against infringers. An informal venture cannot.
