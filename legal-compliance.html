⚠️ The Risk You're Already Living With

In India, trademark rights go to whoever files first — not whoever built the brand first. That means your company name, logo, tagline, and product names can be registered by a competitor today. Without a registered trademark, you have zero automatic legal protection. The cost of filing now is a fraction of the cost of losing a trademark dispute later — and we've seen what that looks like.
