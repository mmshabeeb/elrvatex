⚠️ The High-Stakes Risk You Might Be Living With

In India, trademark rights follow the "first to file" doctrine. That means your company name, mobile app title, logo, and product lines can be legally registered by a copycat or competitor today. Without a registered trademark, you have zero automatic statutory protection. The cost of filing early is a tiny fraction of the catastrophic cost of losing a brand name dispute later.

Step-By-Step

From Public Search to Registration Certificate

How our trademark attorneys guide your mark through the official Trade Marks Registry process.

01

Search & Risk Audit

We perform an in-depth public search on the IP India database, assessing phonetic, visual, and semantic similarities.

02

Application Filing

We draft and e-file Form TM-A with optimal classification. You gain the immediate legal right to display the TM mark.

03

Examination & Defense

If an examination report raises Section 9 or 11 objections, our IP legal counsel drafts formal evidentiary replies.

04

Journal & Certificate

Your mark is published in the Trade Marks Journal. Following the opposition window, your official Certificate is granted.
